## 🌀 Welcome to the Kinetic Flow Lab

There's a certain poetry in motion — the way a body pivots mid-air, the way momentum refuses to die, the way a strafe chain becomes less of a technique and more of a rhythm. **Neo Strafe Training — Kinetic Flow Lab** (NST-KFL for the initiated) is a browser-native training sanctum built for people who treat movement as a craft, not a checkbox.

Where the original *Neo Strafe Training* offered a humble, no-frills playground, the Kinetic Flow Lab takes that same spirit and expands it into a full architectural dojo. Think of it less as a "trainee" and more as a *choreography studio for reflexes*. Every corner of this application exists to sharpen one thing: the seamless marriage of input timing and spatial awareness.

> "Movement is the oldest language. We just built a grammar book for it."

---

## ✨ What Makes the Lab Different

### 🎯 Precision-First Philosophy

Most training tools throw numbers at you and hope something sticks. The Kinetic Flow Lab instead treats every session as a dialogue. Timers adapt, difficulty breathes, and the interface whispers corrections instead of shouting statistics. You're not being graded — you're being *tuned*.

### 🌍 A Truly Multilingual Dojo

Twelve languages ship out of the box, with regional phrasing reviewed by native speakers. From Japanese to Brazilian Portuguese, every string has been hand-polished so the experience feels local, not translated. Language is a movement too — and we don't want it to stutter.

### 📱 Responsive by Design, Not by Afterthought

Screen sizes from a folding phone at 280px to an ultrawide monitor at 3440px have each been treated as first-class citizens. The layout reshuffles, controls reposition under your thumbs, and the rhythm meter never clips. Whether you're on a train or at a tournament desk, the lab fits.

### 🕰️ Support That Never Sleeps

A rotating roster of maintainers and community stewards keeps the issue tracker warm around the clock. Ask a question at 3 AM in Reykjavík or 3 PM in Auckland and a human — not a canned reply — will meet you there.

## 🛠️ The Anatomy of a Session

When you open the lab, you're greeted by three concentric rings. The outer ring is **cadence** — the raw rhythm of your inputs. The middle is **coherence** — how well those inputs land relative to the drifting beat. The innermost is **flow** — the compound score that only lights up when the other two agree for a sustained window.

A session can last as long as you want, but the lab quietly encourages natural stopping points. After roughly seven minutes of flow, the interface softens its edges, dims the peripheral glow, and suggests a stretch. We call it the *breath cue*. It's the only feature that has ever made a user cry — happy tears, we're told.

---

## 🧠 Design Principles Behind the Curtain

1. **Latency Is a Feeling, Not a Number** — We measure in milliseconds but describe in sensations. You'll see "snappy," "buttery," and "molasses" before you ever see "43ms."
2. **Progress Should Be Felt** — A subtle rise in the background gradient tracks cumulative practice. No bar to fill, just a room that grows warmer with time.
3. **Failure Is Data** — Missed chains don't flash red. They dissolve into particles that drift toward your next attempt, literally feeding the next run.
4. **Silence Is a Feature** — The lab is quiet by default. Every sound must earn its right to play.

## 🚀 Getting Into the Flow

The lab is delivered as a single self-contained bundle that runs in any modern browser. There's nothing to unzip into system directories, no daemon to babysit, no registry to massage. You load it, and it lives where browsers live. For the more technically inclined, deployment notes and a static-host configuration guide live in the `docs/` folder — but honestly, most people just open it and start moving.

If you prefer a desktop-shaped experience, the app installs as a progressive web app with its own icon, its own window, and its own life in your dock. It behaves like a native citizen without pretending to be one.

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Community & Conduct

This is a dojo, not a coliseum. We ask that everyone who enters treats others with the same care they'd want shown to a newcomer. The full code of conduct is short enough to read in a coffee break, and it boils down to: *be kind, be curious, be patient with people who are learning what you already know.*

Discussions happen in the Issues tab. Feature requests are welcome but come with a gentle expectation: explain the *feeling* you're chasing, not just the mechanism you want. We build for humans, so we need human context.

## 🧪 Testing & Quality Posture

Every merged change passes through three gates:

1. **Deterministic Timing Tests** — Because a rhythm trainer that drifts is a rhythm trainer that lies.
2. **Cross-Browser Snapshots** — Chromium, Gecko, and WebKit all get their turn in the sun.
3. **Accessibility Audits** — Automated contrast, focus order, and reduced-motion checks run on every PR.

We don't claim perfection. We claim *honesty about imperfection*, which we've found is far more useful.
